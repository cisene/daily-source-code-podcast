Hi, I think the reason that you can't hear the UK audio on your TVs is that the rest of Europe use PAL G/B/H for UHF (5.5mhz audio offset), whereas the UK, South Africa, Ireland and and Hong Kong use PAL-I which has a 6mhz offset for th e the audio. I'm not sure why PAL-I does this, but basically it means that for RF reception you're not going to hear the audio. One way around it might be to get a Freeview (DVB-T) box which will put out SCART (where you get CVBS, audio L/R) which your TV should be able to deal with. Ok, so I rose to the challenge of being a TV nerd, this is what 3 years working for a Set-Top-Box company does for you :)
