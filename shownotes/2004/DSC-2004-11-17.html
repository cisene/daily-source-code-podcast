To: Adam Curry
Just because there's no download doesn't mean it's legal. Streaming would fall into public performance since you're more or less broadcasting copyrighted material to a wide, public audience.
The broadcaster would need a license to do this. The listener would not. I'm assuming the broadcaster would not be Grokster in this case, but the person who actually owns the copyrighted material.
It's pretty ridiculous what obtuse lengths people will go to just so they can get stuff for free when they know quite well that they should be paying for it.
And, no, I am not a lawyer, but I'm pretty sure what I've said above is correct.
