Is mijn podcast ervaring uniek?
Dag Adam,

Ik download al sinds januari je podcast en vind je gebabbel leuk om naar te luisteren, vooral als er Nederlands gesproken wordt.

Ik vraag mij af of er nog mensen zijn die hetzelfde ervaren als ze naar podcasts luisteren, spreek er eens over tijdens je podcast:

I listen a lot to podcasts when I walk to the office and when I work out: running, riding my mountainbike and race-bike, bouldering, ...
I've noticed that locations can trigger my memory about a particular podcast. It happened again today: I rode my mountainbike along a route I take frequently (west of Brussels) and at a certain road crossing I remembered a discussion about a new tablet PC on the Tablet PC Show I had listened to the previous time I rode past that location.
It's a kind of reverse soundseeing: things I see trigger sounds I heard.

Have you experienced this or have people mentioned this to you?

Thanks for the show, en wanneer komt er nog een PPP?

Didier Stevens,

Brussel

Hello,

I'm sure plenty of other nerds will jump on this, but I figured
I'd join the chorus.

The DS is the "Nintendo DS," Nintendo's latest handheld gaming
device. The letters DS refer to the system's Dual Screens, which
allow unique display and input options for games.

The GBA is the GameBoy Advance, which was Nintendo's previous
handheld system, still in wide use by gamers and backward compatible
with original GameBoy games.

Our Boutique Selling community is vast and a big hearted group. Many of our fellow designers that we know have lost their homes and all their possessions.
