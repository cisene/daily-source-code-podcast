Mike Krus
Hi

this is Mike Krus, of newsisfree.com


About the current state of oil supply, there's this thing called Tar
Sands. It's basically oil which was not trapped under a non-porous
layer of rocks, so the liquid & gas part has evaporated, leaving only
tar in sand type soil.

There is loads of that in Canada, more than in Gulf apparent. And we
know exactly where it is. Getting the oil out of that is expensive
though (you can't pump it, it's more like mining), would cost about
$45 a barrel. But with the current price of oil, it would be
economically viable.

Of course, this means more oil related pollution for a longer time,
with additional pollution produced while mining the damn thing...

See en.wikipedia.org—Tar_sands
