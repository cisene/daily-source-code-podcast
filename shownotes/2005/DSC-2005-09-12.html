Cirrus vs Cessna
Cirrus may be a sexy, modern plane, but the biggest problem I am
hearing from other pilots that are looking to buy one is the Cirrus'
longevity.
The Cirrus is a composite airplane. It's value drops over time
whereas the Cessna's does not. I used to fly a 1957 Cessna 182 and
almost none of the major components had been replaced. That won't be
true with a composite plane. I have flown the Katana and the Cirrus
and they are great planes, they just won't stand the test of time as
well. A Cirrus is fun to fly for it's modernness and sexyness, it's
just not an investment. I bagged my last girlfriend with a Cirrus.
Picked her up from work in it for a sunset flight.
Todd
