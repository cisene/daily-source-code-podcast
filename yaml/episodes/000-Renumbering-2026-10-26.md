# Renumbering of 2026-10-26

The episode yaml now follows Adam's own numbering: `001`-`179` as proven in [`000-Numbering-before-180.md`](000-Numbering-before-180.md) (every distinct audio enclosure in the Daily Source Code category before the post Adam called "officially show number 180"), from `180` the numbers stated in the posts, feeds and mp3 tags.

Done by a one-off script (scratchpad), then `update-episodes-from-audio.py`, `update-episodes-from-tags.py` and `update-episodes-from-posts.py` filled the new episodes. A moved episode keeps its content and `podcast:guid`; only `episode`, `itunes:episode`, `podcast:episode` and the `#daily-source-code-...-NNN` placeholders changed. New episodes get `podcast:guid` = UUIDv3 (URL namespace) of the original enclosure url.

Numbers in older notes (Irregularities, Numbering) before this date are the old numbers; translate them with this table.
